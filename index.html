Privacy & Data

All documents, images, and records are stored only on this device, encrypted with a key derived from your PIN. Bersales Secure has no server and does not transmit, back up, or have access to your data. Sharing a document (via Messenger, Viber, email, etc.) uses your device's own share feature — once shared, that copy is no longer protected by Bersales Secure's encryption.

Because there is no cloud backup, losing or resetting this device means your Bersales Secure data cannot be recovered unless you've made your own export. Use "Export Encrypted Backup File" periodically and store the file somewhere safe.
